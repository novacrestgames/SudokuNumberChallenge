Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to play Sudoku Number Challenge. However, certain information is collected automatically through third-party services we use to operate the App.

Automatically Collected Information

The following information may be collected automatically when you use the App:

  • → Device information - model, OS version, unique device identifiers
  • → Gameplay events - levels started, completed, hints used, daily streaks
  • → Crash reports - diagnostic data when the App encounters errors
  • → Ad interaction data - information about ads served and viewed

Information You Provide

The App does not require you to provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. No account creation is required. All game progress is stored locally on your device only.

04

In-App Purchases

Sudoku Number Challenge offers optional in-app purchases including hint token packs and gem packs used to unlock themes. All purchases are optional - the game is fully playable without spending money.

All payment processing is handled entirely and securely by Google Play Billing. We never collect, store, access, or process any payment card details or financial information. Purchase receipts are validated through Google's secure infrastructure.

Purchased items such as hint tokens and gems are stored locally on your device and are non-refundable.

06

Data Storage

All game progress - including completed levels, hint tokens, gems, unlocked themes, and daily challenge streaks - is stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to any server controlled by us and is not accessible to us at any time.

If you uninstall the App, all locally stored game data will be permanently deleted from your device. We recommend completing any in-progress purchases before uninstalling.

Data collected by third-party services such as Firebase and AdMob is retained in accordance with their respective data retention policies, linked above.

09

Security

We take the security of your information seriously. All in-app purchases are processed through Google Play's secure, encrypted billing infrastructure. Game data is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's own security features.

Third-party services we use - Google Firebase, AdMob, and Unity - employ industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We encourage you to keep your device's operating system updated and to use a screen lock to protect your device and its data.
